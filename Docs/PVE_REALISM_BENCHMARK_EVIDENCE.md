# Three-mode target implementation: first Sunbreak benchmark — 2026-09-07

Scope:

Unity presentation, source authoring, shader and documentation. First in-engine
benchmark toward the user's [three-mode artwork direction](Art/MODE_TARGETS_20260907.md).
This is an incremental Leveling pass, not completed high-quality art for all three
modes. Arena, Dungeon and character production remain in the
[implementation sequence](Art/REALISM_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

Source changed:

- `Editor/Authoring/SunbreakBasinAuthoring.cs`: denser small ground tufts, integration
  of the new tree builder, dominant-face physical rock UV projection retained
  after terrain-normal smoothing. Far ridge segments reduce 48 → 36 and tree
  instances three → two per outer edge to keep the original chunk budget.
- New `Editor/Authoring/SunbreakCanopyGeometry.cs`: deterministic tapered trunk,
  branch hierarchy and individually textured leaf sprays; 6,372 triangles near /
  2,518 distant, including wood and double-sided leaves. It emits only editor-time
  buffers that join the existing material-batched meshes.
- `Editor/Authoring/SunbreakScannedSurfaceAuthoring.cs` and
  `Shaders/SunbreakTerrain.shader`: limestone/paving use the already retained
  StoneBrickWall001 albedo/normal and hash-verified packed AO/smoothness. Other
  natural surfaces keep their existing scan bindings and shader cost. This is
  measured material AO, not screen-space occlusion or new scanned geometry.
- `Unity/Presentation/WorldLightingPresenter.cs` and the one mode-selection call
  in `Unity/Gameplay/PrototypeGameController.cs`: PvE-only key intensity 2.05,
  shadow strength .78 and a cached 32²-face mipped daylight reflection at .72.
  The cache is released on teardown; prior reflection/lighting restores exactly.
  Training keeps 2.25/.58 and its prior reflection. Exposure is unchanged.
- New EditMode `SunbreakCanopyGeometryTests` (16), `SunbreakRockProjectionTests`
  (5), `SunbreakMasonryMaterialTests` (6, replacing two old rock-material cases),
  new PlayMode `SunbreakLightingIsolationPlayModeTests` (3), and existing basin/
  world-lighting expectation updates. Total suite increase is 28 cases.
- Subsequent bounded asset trial: new `SunbreakScannedRockAuthoring.cs` and eight
  EditMode cases admit one 5,000-triangle Poly Haven rock03 mesh at Frostbound
  Watch, with its original UVs, normals, proportions and measured 1K PBR maps.
  Source intake `POLYHAVEN-2026-003` retains four unchanged originals, hashes,
  CC0 legal code, provenance and notices. The full six-rock model is never
  instantiated. Nearby procedural rubble is omitted only around this placement.
- This evidence, `Docs/Art/REALISM_IMPLEMENTATION.md`, target/readiness/world docs.

Known caveats:

The actual frames show fuller tree crowns, stronger shadows and differentiated
masonry, but the large flat expanses and rounded procedural shelves still fall
well short of the cinematic target. There are hard ground-to-boundary material
transitions. The repeated character bases, boss and companion remain below the
target's model/animation quality. Existing actor labels and off-screen enemy
cards can overlap HUD elements; larger controls are not a blanket UI acceptance.
The one scanned prop is visibly more natural than the adjacent procedural stones,
but it does not resolve the large-scale composition or foliage/character quality.

Next safe action:

Expand from the single verified scanned-rock trial to one coherent camera-tested
camp: replace the conspicuous rounded formation and empty ground-edge composition
with properly optimized, licensed or original 3D assets, then measure it on the
user's phone before rolling the asset pipeline across the region, Arena and Dungeon.
Do not claim that further procedural density or grading alone completes the art.
