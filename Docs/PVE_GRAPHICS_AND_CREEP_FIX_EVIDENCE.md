Source changed:

- `GrindingRegionRunController.cs`: snapshot-based runtime abandonment gate.
  Previously, moving beyond 45 m from a camp immediately retired all surviving
  participants, even a chasing creep beside the player. Ordinary leash crossing
  now retains the camp while a surviving creep is within 60 m of the player,
  allowing the existing deterministic return-home brain to remain visible.
  Missing snapshots fail closed. The one-camp/three-mob cap, cooldown and unique
  activation IDs remain. Refuge entry deliberately still resets the encounter;
  this is a specific ordinary-leash fix, not a claim to have reproduced every
  possible disappearance report.
- `PrototypeGameController.Grinding.cs`: runtime retirement uses that gate before
  despawning Core actors and returning their existing views to the pool.
- Application and PlayMode regression tests cover near-chaser retention, exact
  distance threshold, distant retirement, returning actors, missing snapshots,
  dead actors, refuge behavior, no rewards from retirement and bounded view reuse.
- `SunbreakBasinAuthoring.cs`: denser whole-source short-grass colonies around the
  focal court, with the fighting centre left open and all walkable detail below
  18 cm. Source positions, UVs and normals are not distorted. Fewer repeated outer
  mounds and procedural perimeter trees free this geometry budget; the continuous
  Core-edge bank and background underlay remain. Chunk caps stay 65,000 triangles
  and 80 renderers.
- `SunbreakRuinMasonryAuthoring.cs`, its new geometry tests and updated courtyard
  contracts: separately named source-derived wall/corner with clipped, capped
  fracture edges and three grounded source offcuts. Original model assets stay
  unchanged. The first native view rejected a stair-like cut; the final profile
  retains two masses separated by a central collapse. 255 triangles and one
  opaque renderer replace the original pair's 1,276 triangles/two renderers.
  Procedure: `SourceArt/Environment/SunbreakRuinMasonryV1/README.md`.
- `SunbreakVistaAuthoring.cs` and tests: five independently placed world-space
  ridgelines replace concentric mountain bands, using unequal summit stations,
  steep inward faces, broad backs and low saddles. The existing 84 woodland trees
  resample the actual new surface. Topology stays 27,042 triangles/four renderers;
  the existing triplanar shader and materials are unchanged.
- Basin boundary banks now share area-weighted lighting normals across coincident
  vertices; positions, UV phase and triangle counts are unchanged. Existing
  generated bank/footprint tests also verify the shared normals.
