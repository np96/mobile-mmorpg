# Provenance

- Classification: original first-party project art.
- Generator: `Tools/Art/Blender/build_fortress_kit.py`.
- DCC: Blender 5.2 LTS.
- External meshes: none.
- External textures: none.
- Scans, photogrammetry, traced franchise symbols, and copied game assets: none.
- Intended style: an original stylized high-fantasy volcanic fortress designed for
  the project's fixed strategy camera and mobile rendering budget.

The geometry is procedurally assembled from newly authored profiles and primitives,
then beveled, UV-unwrapped, triangulated, and exported by the tracked build script.
It is not a derivative of World of Warcraft, Lineage, Mobile Legends, or another
commercial game's asset files.

The wardstone uses an original broad stacked-octagon masonry profile with three
body courses, a wide plinth and cap, and a restrained single ember inset. Its root,
ground pivot, and authoritative gameplay footprint remain unchanged.

The continuous fortress foundation is likewise first-party procedural geometry.
It is revolved from a newly authored sloped retaining-wall profile, then combined
with eighteen original inset bay blocks and eighteen radial buttress feet. No scan,
kitbash, downloaded texture, franchise layout, or commercial arena mesh was used.
Its recessed height and 21.30 m inner radius are presentation constraints that
preserve the project's authoritative lava and kill-radius rules; the FBX carries no
collision or gameplay data.
